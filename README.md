## About Me

I’m **Eduardo Abdala**, an **Embedded Systems Engineer** focused on firmware development, low-level systems, embedded computing, and research-oriented development.

I currently work at **Perto S.A.**, contributing to embedded solutions involving hardware/software integration, debugging, validation, firmware architecture, and real-time systems.

I am also pursuing a **Master’s degree in Embedded Systems** at **UFRGS (Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul)**, where I work on research involving embedded computing, intelligent systems, computer architecture, deep learning, and advanced firmware/software development.

My current research interests include **AI applied to embedded systems**, **embedded security**, and **post-quantum cryptography for embedded platforms**.
